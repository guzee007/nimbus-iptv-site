Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The short version: Nimbus IPTV doesn't collect analytics, doesn't show ads, and doesn't sell your data. Almost everything you do in the app — your playlists, credentials, favorites, and settings — stays on your own computer. The only information we ever see is what's needed to process a Pro purchase.

What stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app's local storage on your own computer, and is never sent to us:

  • Any IPTV server URL, username, or password you enter (Xtream Codes or M3U credentials)
  • Favorites, watch history, resume positions, and app settings
  • Your parental-control PIN and locked categories
  • Your free-trial start date and license activation status

We never see, transmit, or have access to your IPTV provider credentials or the content you watch.

What we collect when you buy Nimbus IPTV Pro

Payment itself is processed entirely by Stripe — we never see or store your card details. Once a purchase succeeds, Stripe shares your email address and payment confirmation with our license system (hosted on Cloudflare), which we use to:

  • Generate your license key
  • Store your email address, license key, and the number of devices it's been activated on — used only to support your purchase (e.g. if you need help recovering a lost key)

We don't use this information for marketing and don't share it with anyone beyond the service providers named here (Stripe for payment, Cloudflare for hosting the license records).

Third-party services

Nimbus IPTV relies on:

  • Stripe — payment processing (Stripe's privacy policy)
  • Cloudflare — hosts the license-verification service (Cloudflare's privacy policy)

Google Fonts are loaded for the app's typefaces, which involves a request to Google's font servers.

Content you connect to the app

Nimbus IPTV is a generic media player. It does not provide, host, or endorse any live TV, movie, or series content. Any server or playlist you add is one you provide yourself, and any data exchanged with that server (your credentials, the streams you request) is between you and that provider — we have no visibility into it.

Children's privacy

Nimbus IPTV is not directed at children. The built-in parental-lock feature is a content-filtering convenience only and does not collect any data about who uses it.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your purchase/license record, contact us via GitHub Issues with your order email, and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: GitHub Issues.
